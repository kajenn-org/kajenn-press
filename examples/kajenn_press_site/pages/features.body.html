Editing

The site tab.

01

Visual editor

The page rendered as it will be published, with slots outlined on request. A click on a slot opens the block editor: CodeMirror, Jodit and a preview.

02

Pages and Contents

A drawer with the pages, their folders and slots, and every content of the site. Drop a content on a slot to place it; marks show pages changed since publication and copies older than their original.

03

One editor at a time

Opening a site takes it for 30 minutes of activity. Others see who holds it and can only look, so nobody overwrites another person's work.

Templates

Layouts in Python, pages in static HTML.

A template defines build(source, data) with Gramlot or HtmlBuilder and marks its slots with kpress_slot. Pages are static HTML that works without JavaScript; a slot can host an iframe, with the content as static fallback.

What a template reads

  • page.title, page.language, page.urls
  • texts.<name>: menu and footer texts, translated
  • assets.<name>.url: stylesheets and images
  • page data from named connections, rendered again when it expires
HTML and Markdown contents pass through the same nh3 allow-list. Scripts, event handlers, iframes and unsafe URL schemes are removed before a content enters a slot.

SEO and languages

Every page, every language.

04

SEO per page

Title, description, canonical, robots, social share, JSON-LD and alt texts, with the root page's defaults; previews of the search result and of the share; checks before Publish.

05

Languages

A main language and others. Each content and text has a translation marked missing, to update or to review; URLs carry the language prefix; pages carry hreflang.

06

Help me

A Sonnet chat that builds pages, offers choices as cards and proposes translations to review. It cannot publish. Tokens and cost show per chat and per site.

Publication and serving

S3 keeps the versions.

Sources and published pages live on two S3 buckets with versioning. Kajenn Press keeps no version history of its own.

Per-page objects

One zip per page and language, with its HTML and assets; a site index pins the version online, so readers never see half a publication.

A Kajenn site server

Reads the index and the site addresses with read-only keys, serves pages, assets, redirects, sitemap and robots, and renders again the pages whose data expires.

Private and maintenance

A site can be private or in maintenance; a signed private link shows the real site to whoever holds it.
